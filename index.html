Why Wizards Rewind exists

A guy, a collection full of VHS tapes, and a refusal to let it just sit there.

Every tape in this vault is something I actually hunted down — thrift stores, Goodwill bins, garage sales — the slow, patient way. Nobody makes new VHS tapes. Every copy that's still out there is one somebody has to find, and once it's found, it deserves to actually get watched, not just sit pretty in a collection.

Streaming gave us everything, all at once, forever — and somewhere in there we lost the part where you had to choose something, carry it home, and actually give it back. That's the whole idea here: a small, real limit (3 at a time), a real return date, and a collection of movies that isn't trying to auto-play the next thing at you.

So this is a genuine, real-deal rental system — not a museum. Borrow tapes, actually watch them, bring them back, tell people what you thought. If enough of you leave reviews, this turns into something closer to a neighborhood video store than a guy's personal collection with a website slapped on it.

The rules, plainly

  • 3 tapes at a time. Enough to have options, not enough to hoard.
  • Series count as one slot. Want all three Indiana Jones tapes? That's one slot, not three — go ahead and take the whole thing.
  • 5 days per rental. Pick a pickup date, tapes are due back 5 days later. Need more time? Just ask — extra days are available for a small fee.
  • Leave a review. Good, bad, whatever — future renters will thank you.
